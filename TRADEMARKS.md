# LibreHR Trademark Policy

> **DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Trademark registration is in progress.

"LibreHR", the LibreHR logo and the "L'Arc" mark (the **Marks**) identify the official LibreHR project and its services. The code licences (AGPL-3.0-or-later and the LibreHR Enterprise License) cover copyright only. They grant **no** rights to the Marks. The CC BY-SA 4.0 licence on the brand image files (`docs/brand.md`) also covers only the copyright in those files, not use of the Marks as a trademark.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your product or service is "based on LibreHR", "compatible with LibreHR" or "a fork of LibreHR".
- Using the Marks to refer to the project in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews.
- Running unmodified official releases for your own organization under the LibreHR name.
- Distributing unmodified official releases, including in Linux distributions and container registries, as long as you do not suggest an endorsement.

## Needs written permission

- Offering LibreHR, or a modified version, as a hosted or managed service under the Marks (for example "LibreHR hosting" or "LibreHR Cloud"). Hosting providers and gestorías can apply to the partner programme; see `docs/editions.md`.
- Using the Marks in a company, product, domain or social media name.
- Merchandise and event names.

## Not allowed

- Distributing a modified version under the Marks. Forks must use a different name and logo.
- Using the Marks in a way that suggests the project endorses you when it does not, or in a misleading or disparaging way.

To ask for permission, contact the maintainers through the channel listed in `SECURITY.md`.
